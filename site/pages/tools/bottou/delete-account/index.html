BOTTOU Account deletion

Delete your account, and everything with it.

Last updated 9 August 2026

You can delete your Bottou account, and all data associated with it, directly in the app. No email, no form, no waiting period.

How to delete your account

  • Open Bottou and sign in
  • Go to Settings
  • Choose Delete account and confirm

What gets deleted

Everything. Your email address, your credentials, and every focus block and reflection stored on the server are permanently deleted immediately. The local data on the device you deleted from is wiped at the same time. There are no backups of deleted accounts and no recovery — deletion is final.

Deleting data without deleting your account

You don't have to delete the whole account to remove data: individual blocks and reflections can be deleted in the app, and Settings → Delete all focus data removes all focus records while keeping your account.

If you can't sign in

Account deletion requires being signed in. If you cannot access your account, contact bottou@systemsatlasproject.com from the email address the account is registered to, and it will be deleted for you.

See also the Bottou privacy policy.
